Stride

Nike-Run-Club-style guided runs. Start wherever you are, finish at 5K. A coach talks you through every walk, every interval, every last thirty seconds. The coach can be you: Stride uses your Apple Personal Voice, or clips you recorded, so the voice in your ears is your own.

  • Ten-week walk/run plan with a Week 0 on-ramp and an honest starting-point check
  • Guided library: First Run, Easy 15, Comeback Run, 5K Day
  • GPS distance and pace, saved to Health as a running workout
  • Coach ducks your music, keeps talking in the background
